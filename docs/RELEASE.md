Marco Polo — A Skyrim Economy Mod, 0.1.4.

Supply contracts now appear only through the optional Missives addon. Read and close their board notes to accept, deliver or cancel orders. The SKSE Menu Framework ledger shows market stocks, prices and diagnostic export, without contract listings or actions. Missives and Marco Polo - Missives.esp are required for new postings.

Household goods now have regional supply, demand, prices and merchant replenishment. This covers clutter-tagged items from Skyrim and other mods, plus explicit mappings for vanilla silver plates, bowls, platters, jugs and candlesticks, linen wraps, charcoal and Hearthfire Extended rugs. Each item's own value remains part of its price. Quest and special-instance protections continue to apply; shop displays are traded without being duplicated or automatically replenished by Marco Polo.

Existing schema-1 economies upgrade without resetting their established markets, merchants, shipments, accepted contracts or time. Only the new household stock is initialized. New saves use schema 2 and require 0.1.4 or later; preserve the original save/co-save pair when testing the update. Install the DLL and both updated economy/classification files together.

Requires Skyrim SE Steam 1.6.1170, matching SKSE/Address Library and a compatible SkyUI barter menu. Exit Skyrim completely before updating the main ZIP. The Missives ESP records are unchanged; its ZIP remains optional. The core needs no ESP or Papyrus scripts.

Automated checks cover migration, rejection of malformed data, simulation, persistence, pricing and display-stock accounting. The 0.1.4 changes still need an in-game check. The preceding 0.1.3 browsing and purchase test passed with Skyrim's original UI-focus sound replacing one MEMOSPORE WAV. The separate exit-to-menu failure and the original affected Markarth saves remain unresolved; this update does not claim to fix them.
