# Game testing for 0.1.4

Automated tests verify deterministic batching/save restoration, invalid-save rollback, inventory conservation, quantity pricing, round-trip pricing, contract escrow/expiry and five simulated years. The Windows build and PE audit check compilation, exports and the runtime gate. **These do not substitute for the checks below.**

For 0.1.4, load a preserved 0.1.3 save and confirm its established market prices, merchant stock and accepted contracts survive. The ledger should gain Household goods and contain no contract listings or buttons. Buy and sell silver plates, ordinary clutter and supported rugs; confirm exact gold, supply response and successful purchases after the chest portion runs out. Save to a new slot, restart Skyrim and reload it. Test Missives actions through their notes, and repeat without the addon to confirm that no new contracts appear. Automated migration tests cover preserved state, repeated round-trips and rejection of incomplete or malformed data.

Local validation also upgraded three backed-up 0.1.3 economy payloads, including a day-7 state with four merchants and eight contracts. After removing only the added household stock and schema change, each matched its original payload exactly; each upgraded payload passed a second exact save/load round-trip. The original `.ess` and `.skse` files were not modified. Linux sanitizer tests, the Windows build and Windows core tests under Wine passed. The menu and clutter changes still require the game checks above.

The 2026-10-24 game session with 0.1.2 (`93be481f`) confirmed corrected single-item quotes, exact stack settlement and a successful fresh-save load after a full restart. The first load attempt crashed and the second succeeded. Save/load stability therefore remains unresolved; see [the engine audit](ENGINE-AUDIT.md) for the evidence and profile limitations. Container Item Distributor compatibility has not yet passed an in-game test.

The original Markarth saves have not been retested with 0.1.2; the successful reload was on the new character. A subsequent 0.1.2 test found blocked books, clothes and mixed-source stacks. Version 0.1.3 corrects the chest-only stock check and accounts for shop-display purchases. Prioritize buying these goods from Belethor, buying a mixed stack until its final item, checking exact gold, and saving/reloading afterward. The physical display must disappear only when Skyrim sells it, and it must not be duplicated into the merchant chest on later visits.

The user confirmed that 0.1.3 permits the previously blocked purchases. A repeatable shop-browsing audio crash also occurred with the DLL absent. Disabling one MEMOSPORE UI-focus WAV in favor of Skyrim's original sound allowed the same saved-shop browsing to pass. With Marco Polo, CDF and SPID restored, the user again reported normal browsing and purchases; 12 priced transactions matched their final gold transfers, including a mod-added book, clothing, external stock and bulk materials. The single sound replacement remains disabled. General save/load stability remains unverified, and the separate exit-to-menu inventory-reset crash persisted in fresh sessions with CDF absent and with SPID absent; Marco Polo was absent in both exit comparisons. Preserve these as separate failures when comparing logs; see the engine audit. Ordinary unclassified clutter retaining its vanilla price is expected behavior, not a failed pricing hook.

Use a separate save and retain both its `.ess` and `.skse` files. Enable `debugLogging` in `settings.json` before starting Skyrim. Start with the core, matching SKSE/Address Library and SkyUI; then add optional integrations and other economy mods one at a time.

When updating from 0.1.0, exit Skyrim completely before replacing the DLL: that build intercepted the wrong engine function. First try loading the affected save without overwriting it. Logs should name version 0.1.4 and report `Loaded economy`. A failed in-game load is not proof of damaged save bytes; preserve both files before attempting any cleaning. Logs now rotate instead of being erased at startup. With Container Item Distributor installed, expect `existing virtual hook chained`, then successful barter attachment after opening a shop.

1. **Startup:** confirm `MarcoPolo.log` names 1.6.1170, loads configuration and installs barter integration. Load an existing save and open a merchant. Ensure no crash or unsupported-menu notification. Repeat with your actual UI replacer.
2. **Quotes:** record player and merchant gold. Buy and sell one ordinary ingot, then a stack of 20. Check list/card values, the selected quantity total and exact gold changes. Move the slider both directions; cancel it. Cancelling must not alter stocks or cash. Repeat near an affordability limit, after a Speech increase, with relevant perks and enchanted/tempered items.
3. **Inventory:** buy a shop's ordinary stock out, wait one and then three game days, return and compare. Supply can constrain replenishment. Test vanilla reset timing, sleeping/fast travel, saving in a shop and merchant death. Verify unique, quest, enchanted and named instances are not replaced or duplicated. Test theft from a registered chest.
4. **Regional effects:** sell a large supply of one commodity and observe its price fall. Compare the same good in another hold. Check stock and price after several days and after long waits. Verify reaching a location inside each hold resolves to the correct market.
5. **Save isolation:** save, trade, reload and compare the restored state. Repeat across two characters and through the main menu. Load-order changes must resolve stable form keys. Keep both save files when transferring a test save.
6. **Missives:** enable the addon. Each original capital board should contain a market report. After shortages develop, read and close a supply order, accept, deliver the exact item near that board and verify exact quantity removal and reward. Try a partial order, delivery in another hold, repeat delivery, cancellation, expiry and reloading an accepted order. Copies show the current posting; the accepted contract's terms stay fixed.
7. **Coexistence:** repeat board checks with Notes and Announcements and Tessera Missives enabled. Their notes/jobs must remain. Check board replacers that retain references. Then repeat trade checks with your other merchant/perk mods.
8. **Optional ledger:** verify it appears only when Menu Framework is installed, opens through your usual Menu Framework/Risa entry, shows markets without any contract listings/actions, and exports a readable diagnostic file. Repeat without Menu Framework or Missives: core trading should still function, and contracts should require the Missives addon.

Report the exact core/archive version, UI/economy mods, merchant/hold, item, count, quoted total, gold before/after, game day, and steps. Include `MarcoPolo.log` and the exported state. The first game pass should prioritize quotes, gold settlement, merchant reset behavior and loading saves; they are the engine-facing boundaries.
